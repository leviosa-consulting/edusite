About Ashford

A school with history, built for the future

For a century, Ashford Academy has held to one conviction: that a great education shapes both mind and character.

Our Mission

Character. Curiosity. Excellence.

These three words have guided Ashford since our founding — and they still shape every decision we make.

We prepare students not merely for the next exam or the next school, but for lives of purpose. Our graduates leave Ashford as confident thinkers, generous collaborators, and citizens who take responsibility for the communities they join.

That mission is possible because of our scale: with fewer than 600 students across thirteen grades, every child is known by name — by teachers, by coaches, and by our head of school.

"We teach students, not just subjects."

Our History

A century in the making

From a one-building schoolhouse to a twelve-acre campus, Ashford's story is one of steady, deliberate growth.

  1. 1924

    Ashford Academy opens its doors with 40 students and four teachers in the original Maplewood schoolhouse.

  2. 1958

    The Upper School is established, extending Ashford's program through grade twelve.

  3. 1987

    The Whitfield Auditorium and arts wing open, anchoring a new commitment to the performing arts.

  4. 2012

    Launch of the STEM & Innovation Center, home to robotics, engineering, and computer science.

  5. 2024

    Ashford celebrates its centennial year and unveils a ten-year vision for the next generation of learners.
