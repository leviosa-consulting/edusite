Why Ashford

An education measured in more than grades

We believe excellent schooling shapes character as much as intellect.

  • Every student known. Advisory groups of eight ensure no one slips through the cracks.
  • Depth over drill. Seminar-style classes built on discussion, projects, and real inquiry.
  • Character in practice. Service learning and honor code responsibilities at every grade level.
  • Room to explore. Arts, athletics, robotics, debate — students are encouraged to try everything.
