We look for curious, kind students — and we make the process as personal as the school itself.

How to Apply

Four steps to enrollment

Applications for the 2027–28 school year open September 1 and are due January 15. Decisions are mailed in March.

Our admissions team reads every application personally. We are happy to talk with your family at any point in the process — no question is too small.

Ask the Admissions Office

  1. Inquire & visit

    Attend an open house or schedule a personal tour to see the school in session.

  2. Submit your application

    Complete the online application with transcripts and two teacher recommendations.

  3. Interview & visit day

    Applicants spend a morning with their prospective class; parents meet with admissions staff.

  4. Decision & enrollment

    Admissions decisions arrive in March; enrollment contracts are due in April.

Common Questions

Admissions FAQ

When can my family tour the campus?

Personal tours run Tuesday through Friday mornings throughout the school year, and we host open houses each fall. Use the contact page to schedule a time that suits you.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. Ashford awards more than $2 million in need-based aid annually, with grants covering up to 90% of tuition. Aid applications are reviewed separately from admissions decisions.

Do you accept mid-year transfers?

When space allows, we welcome mid-year applicants, particularly for families relocating to the area. Contact the admissions office to check availability in your child's grade.

What testing is required?

Lower School applicants complete a playful readiness screening on their visit day. Middle and Upper School applicants take a standard admissions assessment, offered on campus at no cost.

Is transportation provided?

Ashford runs morning and afternoon bus routes serving Maplewood and surrounding towns, along with supervised before- and after-school care from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

Ready to take the first step?

Applications for 2027–28 open September 1. We can't wait to meet your family.
